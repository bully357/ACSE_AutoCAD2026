┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  🛠️ WHY TWO LOCATIONS?                                       │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Your project has a POST-BUILD EVENT (in ACSE.AutoCAD2026.csproj lines 68-70):

<Target Name="PostBuild" AfterTargets="PostBuildEvent">
  <Exec Command="xcopy /Y /I &quot;$(TargetDir)*.dll&quot; &quot;BUNDLE&quot;" />
</Target>

This COPIES the DLL to deploy\ folder after building.

The problem:
  - Sometimes the copy fails silently
  - Sometimes Windows caches the old DLL in deploy\
  - The deploy\ DLL might be from an OLD build

Always use bin\Debug\net8.0-windows\ for testing!
